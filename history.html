OUR HISTORY

Manchester United was founded in 1878 as Newton Heath LYR Football Club. In 1902, the club was renamed and began its journey to becoming one of the biggest football clubs in the world.

Under Sir Alex Ferguson, the club experienced its most successful era, winning numerous Premier League titles and European trophies.

Today, Manchester United continues to build its legacy with passionate fans, world-class players, and unforgettable moments at Old Trafford.
